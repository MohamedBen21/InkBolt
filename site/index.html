How Fixation Reading Works

Three simple principles grounded in reading mechanics.

01 / Mechanic

Eye Fixation Anchoring

When you read, your eyes do not glide continuously. They hop between fixation points (saccades). By bolding the lead letters of words, Inkbolt gives your eye an instant landing spot so your brain can decode the word without scanning every letter.

02 / Typography

Zero Layout Shift

Inkbolt only changes font-weight on a few letters per word. It never alters your site's font-family, font size, line spacing, or margins. The layout of the webpage or book remains completely untouched.

03 / Privacy

Entirely Local & Offline

Everything happens inside your browser. No text is analyzed remotely, no files are transmitted, and no account is required. It works equally well when your machine is completely offline.

Skim Search Results & Articles Fast

When searching Google, DuckDuckGo, or reading long articles and technical documentation, your eye naturally glides past filler text straight to the core concepts.

Questions & Answers

Does Inkbolt alter my fonts or page layouts?

Never. Inkbolt strictly bumps the font-weight on the first few letters of each word using an inline style. The font-family, font size, margins, and page structure are left completely untouched.

Is this the "Bionic Reading" app?

No. Inkbolt is an independent open-source implementation of fixation-guided reading. "Bionic Reading" is an active registered trademark of Bionic Reading GmbH; Inkbolt is its own independent product, unaffiliated.

Which e-book formats can I convert?

EPUB, PDF, and TXT files directly. EPUB stays an authentic EPUB with chapters and cover art intact. PDF maintains the original layouts and diagrams with bold overlays drawn in place. TXT uses real Unicode bold characters.

Does it send my text or documents anywhere?

No. Everything runs strictly inside your local browser runtime. No server requests, no tracking, and no external dependencies.
